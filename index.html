Consolidating 185 Airflow DAG files into 97

Our lakehouse refresh took two to three hours, mostly spent starting Spark applications. Folding them into a few config-driven jobs got it down to minutes.

 November 2026 · 3 min read

An Iceberg MERGE that read 930 million rows to write 316 thousand

The merge condition matched on year, month and day, so I assumed it only touched one day. Spark was reading the whole table every hour.

 November 2026 · 3 min read

A Spark 3.5 optimizer crash that a cache was hiding

A production job failed on every retry with an internal Spark error. The cause was a nullif() join key, and a persist() had been hiding it.

 November 2026 · 3 min read

How ignoreCorruptFiles hid missing days in our lakehouse

Backfills kept losing days while every run reported success. A Spark read option was skipping files on transient errors.

 November 2026 · 3 min read

Ingesting our PagerDuty history in 18 minutes instead of two hours

The first version crawled the API one incident at a time for up to two hours. The faster one then ran into Spark's 128 MB message limit.
